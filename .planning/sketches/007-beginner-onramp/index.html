How does a beginner see the easiest course and pick their level without feeling overwhelmed, right where they practice, with advanced tiers hidden until reached? Three variants below. Use the simulator to see how each looks at different points of progress.

Today (the problem)

The level control is six bare tier-number buttons (1 to 6, with 5 and 6 disabled) buried in the Progress page settings. A beginner never sees a name, a description, or a reason to start at 1. It reads as a settings knob, not a course.

Simulate progress: reached up through tier

Controls all three variants below. This stands in for drill history the real app already tracks.

Drills

Only the current tier and the immediate next tier ever show. Everything past that is not rendered at all, not even as a locked row, until you get closer to it. "Start practicing" is the only button that matters on this page.

Drills

Every reached tier is tappable and switches your practice level immediately. Future tiers sit dimmed so you know the ladder keeps going. Tier 5 and 6 do not appear at all until tier 4 is reached, then show up as locked, dashed rungs, "coming later."
