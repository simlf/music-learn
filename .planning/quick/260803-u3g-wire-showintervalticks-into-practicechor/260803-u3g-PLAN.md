Wire PianoKeyboard's already-shipped `showIntervalTicks` prop into PracticeChordDrill's three PianoKeyboard instances, gated on `answered` exactly the way the neighbouring `showIntervalBadges` prop already is.

Adding the rail answers the complementary question ("how far apart are these two notes") at the same moment, on the same board, for free.

Output: three prop additions in PracticeChordDrill.svelte, one narrowed existing assertion, and new mode-by-mode test coverage.
