<objective>
Unify the two separate "From my songs" practice-pool filters (Recall's `music-learn:recall-song-filter` and Drills' `music-learn:drills-song-filter`) into ONE global preference, set from a single control on the /songs page. Recall and Drills stop rendering their own toggle and simply read the persisted preference.

Purpose: The filter is a single user intent ("practice only what's in my songs") that today drifts between two independent per-page switches. Consolidating it removes the confusion and puts the control where the user manages their songs.

Output: A shared `songFilterPref` module (single key + first-read migration), a new global filter control on /songs, and Recall/Drills reduced to read-only consumers. The `drawFromSymbols`/`drawFromPool` seam (Phase 18-02) and the empty-active-songs fallback behavior are preserved on both consumers.
</objective>
