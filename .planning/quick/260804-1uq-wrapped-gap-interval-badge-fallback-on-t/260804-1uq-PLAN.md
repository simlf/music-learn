<objective>
Make the keyboard carry EVERY adjacent-tone gap, then drop the now-redundant CountingChipRow from PracticeChordDrill's feedback block.

Purpose: today `intervalGaps` silently DROPS any gap whose arrival note does not render strictly to the right of its departure note, and suppresses the whole rail on a stacked two-row board. On a one-octave phone board a Gm renders D to the LEFT of the root, so the user sees one of the chord's two intervals and has no way to know a second one exists. The guard is correct — drawing that line leftwards would span five white keys for a major third, a false distance — so the fix is a different RENDERING for those gaps (a badge on the arrival key), not a weaker guard. Once the keyboard carries note letters (engraved), role (corner badges) and every gap (rail + badges), the practice drill's CountingChipRow is pure duplication with no tap targets, and goes.

Output: a `showIntervalTicks`-gated arrival-key badge in PianoKeyboard (+ its own tests), the CountingChipRow call site removed from PracticeChordDrill, one orphaned i18n key deleted, and the practice-drill tests re-pointed at the new invariant.
</objective>
