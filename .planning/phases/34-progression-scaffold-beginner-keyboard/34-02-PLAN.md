<objective>
Deliver SCAF-01: an escalating, optional counting-hint ladder inline in `ProgressionDrill.svelte` that unblocks a beginner on the earn-the-symbol progression drill without ever revealing the chord symbol, and that fades as the current bar's FSRS card matures. The ladder reuses the Phase 32 `CountingChipRow` primitive, mirrors `HintLadder.svelte`'s proven escalating interaction and CSS (without extending that shared component), and lights the shipped `ghostKeys` on the live play keyboard as its deepest rung.

Purpose: The original earn-the-symbol drill hard-blocked a beginner who could not derive the chord from the roman numeral alone (sketch 024). An optional rung-by-rung ladder gives a way to climb while keeping the symbol earned.

Output: `ProgressionDrill.svelte` renders a single "Afficher un indice" control during the play step; each tap reveals the next rung (degree -> root, then the CountingChipRow, then ghost keys). The rung count offered follows the `stage` signal from Plan 34-01 (meet 3, subtract 2, drill 0). New tests prove the reveal sequence, symbol-hidden invariant, maturity fade, cursor-reset behavior, and REG-01 non-regression.
</objective>
