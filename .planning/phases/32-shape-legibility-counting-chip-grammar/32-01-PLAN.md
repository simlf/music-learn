<objective>
Ship the counting-chip grammar as a reusable primitive and wire it into the name-it (nomme cet accord) feedback surface, replacing the illegible grey uppercase mono shape line (`M7: M3 · M3 · M3`) with a row of role-tinted tone chips separated by fill/outline gap chips plus a semitone-count legend (SHAPE-01, sketch 021-A + counting-hint-grammar.md).

Purpose: A beginner must read a chord's third-pattern as a shape (small-big-small = m7) without decoding grey mono letters. This chip grammar is also the shared visual primitive that Phases 34 (progression hint ladder) and 35 (inversion hint ladder) reuse, so it ships FIRST as a standalone component — mirroring how v2.2 shipped IntervalView (Phase 26) before its consumers.

Output: A new `CountingChipRow.svelte` component (the `.chiprow` / `.tone-chip` / `.gap-chip` grammar), its component test, new legend i18n keys in drills.ts, and PracticeChordDrill wired to it with the old shape line removed.
</objective>
