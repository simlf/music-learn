<objective>
Refactor ShellVoicingDrill.svelte so the voicing drill drops its always-on 3-way `Fond. · Shell · Sans fond.` voicing-style selector (the drill-stage `voicing-selector` chip strip) and instead starts on the shell, reaching the full voicing via a "+ poser la fondamentale" nudge and the rootless voicing via a "− retirer la basse" nudge. Style becomes a by-product of what you add/remove, shown as a current-voicing label, not a mode you pick up front.

This implements VOICE-01 (sketch 022, winner variant A: add/drop nudges — see .planning/sketches/022-voicing-lean-flow/README.md + index.html).

Scope fence: ONLY the drill-stage mode chrome changes. The staged-by-card-state step indicator (1 Meet / 2 Subtract / 3 Drill), the meet compare intro, the subtract ghost-hatch stage, the from-memory chroma grading (matchChord), the deck-maturity staging (voicingStageForChord), and the embedSymbol/onComplete embed seam all stay exactly as they are (REG-01). No other drill surface is touched.

Purpose: the 3-way toggle read as heavy chrome ("cassée et pas très intuitive", UAT V5). Removing it and blending style variation into contextual nudges makes the drill leaner while preserving deliberate practice of all three voicings.

Output: a leaner ShellVoicingDrill drill stage; updated voicings i18n domain; nudge-flow test coverage; the milestone suite stays green.
</objective>
