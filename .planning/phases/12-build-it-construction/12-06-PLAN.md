<objective>
Wire Phase 12's construction outcomes into the Phase 11 memorization engine (BUILD-04, D-09): when a construction session completes, record a gradeable event into the fact's `formula-build` mode card via `deck.ts`'s `getCard`/`recordGrade`, mapping the session's wrong-tap count to an FSRS rating, and surface the write with a status line on the completion summary.

Purpose: this closes the loop the whole phase was built for — construction accuracy now feeds the SAME spaced-repetition scheduler Phase 11 built, as a distinct mode of the fact rather than a separate system. Chord targets deliberately reuse the existing `chord-formula` factId (so a chord's recall card and its construction card are two views of the same underlying fact), while scale targets use the new `scale-formula` factId from 12-01 (since Phase 11's `scale-degree` facts are the wrong grain for "the whole scale as one buildable unit").
Output: BuildSession.svelte's FSRS write on completion, its rating-mapping function, the "SAVED TO RECALL · FORMULA-BUILD MODE" status line, and comprehensive mocked-deck test coverage.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-12-10 | Tampering | recordConstructionOutcome's rating argument | low | mitigate | ratingForWrongTaps is a pure, total function over a numeric wrongTapCount (itself only ever incremented by construction.ts's own grading, never user-supplied directly) mapping to one of three fixed Rating enum values; there is no path for an out-of-range or attacker-controlled rating to reach recordGrade. |
| T-12-11 | Denial of Service | the async getCard/recordGrade write | low | mitigate | The entire write is wrapped in try/catch and fired without awaiting inside the render path (fire-and-forget), so a slow/failed IndexedDB write can never block or hang the completion summary's render. |
