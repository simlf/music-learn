<objective>
Extend two small, load-bearing seams the Phase 12 UI-SPEC identified but deliberately left unresolved: a plain-English consecutive-interval phrasing helper in the theory engine (`phraseStep`, D-03), and the two Phase 11 SRS data-layer seams construction outcomes need to attach to (`deck.ts`'s `Mode` union gaining `'formula-build'`, and a scale-level factId shape via `facts.ts`'s `scaleFormulaFactId`, D-09).

Purpose: every later Phase 12 plan depends on these seams — the construction engine and BuildSession need `phraseStep` for step instructions, and the FSRS integration plan needs `Mode`/`FactKind` widened plus a single-card lookup before it can persist a construction outcome. Getting the seam boundaries right here (especially NOT polluting the existing 192-fact catalog) is what keeps Phase 11's RecallSession/RecallMap from silently breaking later.
Output: `interval.ts`'s `phraseStep`, `facts.ts`'s widened `FactKind` + `scaleFormulaFactId`, `deck.ts`'s widened `Mode`/schema + `getCard`, all unit-tested including explicit non-regression proofs for Phase 11's existing behavior.
</objective>
