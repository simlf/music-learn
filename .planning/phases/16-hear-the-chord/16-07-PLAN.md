<objective>
Fix the two audio-layer defects in the study-mode arpeggio primitive `playArpeggio`
(both confirmed in `src/lib/audio/audio.ts:274-285`):

1. Gap 2 / WR-02 — it voices every note flat at `toPitch(note, 4)` while `playChord`
   and the keyboard use the ascending-octave walk `toAscendingPitches`. For a scale
   (e.g. G major) the roll audibly descends mid-sequence and no longer matches the
   pitch its lit key represents.
2. Gap 1 (audio half) / WR-01 — it has no start-delay parameter, so note 0 always
   attacks at t=0. The consuming visual timers in BuildSession are offset by 260ms on
   the initial reveal, producing a fixed 260ms audio-leads-visual desync.

This plan changes ONLY the audio primitive and its unit tests. Wiring the new
`startDelayMs` through BuildSession and fixing the letter-keyed pending collapse is
plan 16-08 (wave 2), which depends on this plan.

Purpose: make the rolled pitches equal the struck pitches, and give the caller the
knob it needs to align audio with the delayed visual roll.
Output: an ascending-voiced, delay-capable `playArpeggio` plus unit tests that prove
both behaviors.
</objective>
