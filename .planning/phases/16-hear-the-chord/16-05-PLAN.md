<objective>
Wire the core BUILD reveal (HEAR-01, plus HEAR-02's sound-off pulse): when a construction session reaches the `complete` state, the correct chord auto-plays once via the existing `playChord` engine the instant the answer stamps in, the lit keys and a newly-rendered serif `ChordGlyph` pulse once in time, and a ghost "Hear again" control replays on demand. With sound off, the same reveal still runs the visual pulse and never dead-ends.

Purpose: This is the phase's central deliverable. It reuses Plan 16-01's audio/keyframe primitives and Plans 16-02/03's pulse-capable components, and it strictly honors the locked reveal constraints (pulse is a separate one-shot from stamp-in; pulse lives outside the sound gate; Hear again is never disabled). The study-mode arpeggio roll is layered on in Plan 16-06.
Output: The `complete`-block reveal wiring in `BuildSession.svelte` and extended `buildSession.test.ts`.
</objective>
