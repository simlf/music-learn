<objective>
Implement the locked Sketch 001 "Colors + Gray" (Variant A) merged feedback keyboard (USER DIRECTIVE). Today a wrong construction answer renders two keyboards (the input keyboard plus a separate correct-answer keyboard) and a "You played: / Correct:" text diff. Collapse that into ONE keyboard that transforms in place on Check: the correct chord fills with the locked interval colors (root/third/fifth/seventh), the user's wrong extra notes fill a warm gray with a small ✕, and a one-line legend explains the two meanings. No played-vs-missed distinction (the answer is the message). Reuse the existing stamp-in animation and respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Purpose: Land the user's validated sketch decision as the drills wrong-answer feedback, simpler to read and far better on a phone (one keyboard, no wasted vertical space). Depends on 09-01 so the merged keyboard already wraps correctly at one octave.
Output: New wrong-answer tokens + shared stamp keyframe in app.css, a `wrongLetters` feedback mode on PianoKeyboard, and the rewritten construction feedback block in the drills page.

Covers: Sketch 001 (user directive), reuses D-02 (one-octave wrapping) from 09-01. Scope is the CONSTRUCTION wrong-answer branch only; the identification reveal (display keyboard + ChordGlyph) is unchanged.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** phone-first pianist, **I want to** see one keyboard that shows the right chord and my wrong notes at a glance, **so that** a mistake teaches me the shape instantly without splitting my attention across two keyboards.
