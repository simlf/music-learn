<objective>
Fix issue #14 point 2: on a phone in French the bottom tab bar takes too much vertical space because the full French labels (Rechercher, Exercices, Révision, Morceaux, Plus) are long. Purpose: Reclaim vertical space on the phone and make the nav language-independent in height, so French no longer eats the viewport.
Output: Icon + micro-label tab items in +layout.svelte, a 56px bar with the --tab-bar-clearance token updated, and masthead tests covering the icon+label structure and accessible names.
</objective>
