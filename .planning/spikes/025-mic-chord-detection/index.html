how to read it

Green bars are the pitch classes the target chord expects. Red bars are pitch classes that should not be there. A verdict needs every green bar above 0.20, every red bar below 0.25, and the weakest green beating the strongest red by 0.15, held for 3 consecutive frames (about 460 ms). Chroma is octave blind on purpose: playing the chord an octave lower reads identically. Echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain are all requested off, because phone voice processing mangles a piano.
