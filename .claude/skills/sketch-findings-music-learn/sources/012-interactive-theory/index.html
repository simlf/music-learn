Sketch 012 · Interactive theory lessons

The /theory page is static reading. A returning classical pianist with beginner theory needs interactive lessons that build understanding and hand off cleanly into their first drill. How should a lesson look and flow? Worked example across all three: the major third vs the minor third — the interval that makes a chord happy or sad.

Today (the problem)

The /theory route renders prose and a static IntervalScaffold diagram. You read that a major third is four half-steps, nod, and nothing checks whether your hands know it. There is no bridge from "I read this" to "I can find it," and no path into the beginner course on /drills.

The lesson is a vertical stack of tiny steps. Each pairs one plain sentence with one thing to do on the keyboard. Do it right and the step locks green and the next opens. Ends by handing you into a drill.
