Sketch 003 — Distance-from-root badge

Every highlighted key already carries its interval COLOR. Add its interval-from-root as a readable badge. Three placements.

Badge pinned to the top of each colored key. Compact, always attached to its note, but competes with the NoteLabel for the same key space.

Badge stacked under the letter + solfège, inside the key. Reads as one unit (name over interval) but needs vertical room, tight on black keys.

Badge floats above the keyboard on a tick, never crowding the key face. Clearest read of the interval stack, costs a strip of height above the keys.
